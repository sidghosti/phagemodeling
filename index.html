The model
?Phase I runs with phage mutation on. Phase II assumes no further phage mutation, so μV = 0 — the mutation arrow leaves the diagram, and the μV factor and term leave the equations.

Arrow colors match the equation terms, and hovering over either highlights both (green indicates identical expression, yellow indicates related or analogous). Dashed arrows represent a one-latent-period delay in the DDEs. Free phage feed back into new infections, so every attachment splits two ways: the ε share becomes a productive infection, the rest leaves as nonproductive adsorption. Bacteria carrying phage in a nonproductive infection are not affected.

Subscripts: S and R name the bacterial strain, WT and M the phage genotype. Adsorption uses α; decay keeps δ rather than d, which would read ambiguously beside dV/dt.

Infected cells are metabolically inactive, so they are absent from ψ and neither grow nor consume nutrients; the total cell count can briefly exceed K during a wave of infection. Infections before t = 0 are set to zero, so no bursts occur until the first latent period has elapsed. Phage adsorb only to susceptible cells, so resistant cells are a lack of gain rather than a phage sink.
